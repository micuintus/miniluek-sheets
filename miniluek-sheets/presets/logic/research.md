# Research behind the logic lane

What each ice-palace sheet practises and which study it rests on. No study has tested
miniLÜK, story pictures or these sheets; every row borrows evidence from studies of the skill,
and the ground is weaker than the numbers lane's.

Evidence types: **[M]** meta-analysis or systematic review, **[R]** randomised or controlled
trial, **[C]** correlational or cohort study, **[G]** guideline or practice guidance, **[E]**
experiment, descriptive or single-case study, book or narrative review.

## Per sheet

| Sheet | Practises | Design | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Necklaces | continuing an AB pattern | A B A B and an empty ring last; two charms differ in colour and outline | patterning at 4 predicted fifth-grade math (Rittle-Johnson et al. 2017 [C], 517 children); P1 practises extending, the two patterning trials under Limits also taught copying, translating and naming the unit of repeat |
| P2 Palace windows | the same shape in another colour and size | six shapes; the answer is the shape in gold, smaller than its window | children of 3 to 6 tell shapes apart mainly by visual matching (Clements et al. 1999 [E], 97 interviews); P2 varies colour and size, not orientation |
| P3 Winter party | odd one out by kind | three guests alike, one of another kind; every odd guest is also a majority guest on another bench | oddity and seriation lessons improved those skills more than letter, number or art lessons, and the children identified letters better than the number and art groups (Kidd et al. 2012 [R], 72 Head Start children) |
| P4 Castle maps | the same place in a 3 by 3 grid | the princess in one window of nine; the answer is a plain map with a star | spatial training works (Uttal et al. 2013 [M], g = 0.47) and moves math a little, less at preschool age (Hawes et al. 2022 [M], g = 0.28); spatial memory predicted math in children aged 7 to 12 (Crocker et al. 2015 [C]); P4 matches a place between two visible layouts on paper |

## Limits

- Two patterning trials improved patterning but not number skills (Wijns et al. 2021 [R],
  quasi-experimental, 147 children; Zippert et al. 2021 [R], randomised, 211 children). The
  practice guide rates its geometry and patterns recommendation as minimal evidence (Frye et al.
  2013 [G]). The lane practises its own skills and promises nothing for numbers.
- Kidd et al. 2012 taught oddity together with seriation, with toy animals over most of a school
  year, and comes from one research group.
- The patterning, oddity and shape studies tested children aged 3 to 6, partly from low-income
  families.
- Half a sheet per sitting (the pink or the blue six) is a design choice, untested.
